1. Executive Summary

Every day, over 4 million passenger trips are taken across Delhi's public bus fleet. While journey planners like Google Maps or One Delhi efficiently route passengers by scheduled travel time and number of interchanges, they remain blind to passenger comfort. Commuters routinely watch three consecutive packed buses pass them by or endure crushing standee conditions when an alternative route leaving 10 minutes later would offer open seats.

CrowdWise is a production-grade, crowdedness-aware bus journey planner built specifically on the Delhi Transport Stack. Leveraging 2.56 million electronic ticketing machine (ETM) records from October 2026 and static GTFS schedule feeds, CrowdWise reconstructs corridor passenger loads and trains a machine learning inference engine. It presents commuters with intuitive comfort badges—Seats Available, Standing Room, or Crowded—and lets them balance commute speed against comfort.

2. Problem Statement Understanding & Real-World Complexities

Challenge Real-World Transport Reality CrowdWise Solution
Tickets ≠ Occupancy A ticket only records boarding point (and destination). A running passenger count requires grouping by vehicle trip. Vehicles are grouped by trip run and sequenced across pattern stops. A recursive physical mass-conservation equation is solved: Ls = Ls-1 + bs - as, enforcing terminal clearance Lend = 0.
Unrecorded Riders (Pass Holders) Female commuters travel free with Pink Passes; daily and monthly pass holders don't buy point-to-point tickets. A route-specific pass inflation multiplier factor γr ∈ [1.20, 1.45] is incorporated into boarding demand, calibrated against Delhi Transport Department fare revenue audit ratios.
Alighting Uncertainty Riders may alight earlier or later than their printed ticket destination. A hybrid alighting model balances ticketed alightings with destination-gravity decay, preventing artificial passenger accumulations.
Bus Capacity Variations A passenger load of 60 means open seats on an articulated 12-meter low-floor bus, but crushing overcrowding on a 9-meter midi bus. Physical passenger counts are mapped into the Relative Crowding Index (RCI), normalized to vehicle capacity (Vcap = 70 for standard 12m, Vcap = 45 for 9m midi buses).
Sparse Off-Peak Data Night hours (11 PM - 5 AM) or peripheral feeder routes have low ticket counts. CrowdWise outputs an explicit 3-level confidence rating (High, Medium, Low) derived from tree prediction spread and historical sample support.

4. Machine Learning & Calibrated Comfort Taxonomy

4.1 Chronological Split & Leakage Invariant

To prevent look-ahead bias, datasets are split chronologically with a 70% Train, 15% Validation, and 15% Test split. A strict programmatic assertion guarantees:

max(train_timestamp) < min(val_timestamp) < min(test_timestamp)

4.2 Model Performance

  • Spearman Rank Correlation: 0.9297 (exceptional ranking fidelity).
  • Macro-Averaged F1 Score: 0.8083 across all 3 comfort categories.
  • Weighted Absolute Percentage Error (WAPE): 0.2570 (25.7%).
  • Mean Absolute Error (MAE): 1.50 passengers per stretch.

4.3 Relative Crowding Index (RCI) Taxonomy

  • ● Seats Available (RCI < 50): High probability of securing an open seat.
  • ● Standing Room (50 ≤ RCI < 85): Seats occupied, but comfortable standing room.
  • ● Crowded (RCI ≥ 85): Crushed conditions; slow boarding; difficult movement.

5. Commuter Journey Planner & Multi-Objective Optimization

Commuters can select between three clear modes:

  1. Fastest Mode: Prioritizes shortest commute time.
  2. Balanced Mode (Default): 50/50 balance between travel duration and comfort.
  3. Least Crowded Mode: Maximizes chance of a seat, bounded by a strict detour safety cap (T ≤ 1.5 × Tfastest + 10 min).

6. Dual-Audience Value

For Commuters: 1-second comfort recognition, instant stop search autocomplete, Leaflet polyline preview, and transparent "Why this route?" trade-off calculations.

For Bus Operators & Planners: Corridor heat strips across 8 zones, 24-hour diurnal curves (Weekday vs Saturday vs Sunday), and network analytics highlighting corridors that urgently need extra buses.

7. Data Privacy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

  • Salted HMAC-SHA-256 Hashing: Irreversibly hashes all smartcard IDs and ETM transaction IDs.
  • Minute Timestamp Truncation: Drops second-level timestamps to eliminate micro-tracking.
  • Zero PII Persistence: No personal or financial identifiers are retained in the database.
