CrowdWise Technical Note: Mathematical Formulations & Architecture

Transport Stack Hackathon 2026 — Track 2: Passenger Demand & Crowdedness Modeling

1. Demand Reconstruction Mathematics

1.1 Physical Mass Conservation Recurrence

A fundamental axiom of transit demand modeling is that ticket sales represent point events, not instantaneous vehicle occupancy. To determine the passenger load $L_s$ onboard a bus operating along a stop sequence $s \in \{0, 1, \dots, N-1\}$:

$$L_s = L_{s-1} + b_s - a_s \quad \forall s \in \{1, \dots, N-1\}$$

Subject to physical boundary invariants:

  1. Initial Boundary Condition: $L_0 = b_0$ (passenger load begins at the first stop's boardings).
  2. Terminal Boundary Clearance: $L_{N-1} = 0$ (all passengers must alight at terminal).
  3. Physical Non-Negativity Invariant: $L_s \ge 0 \quad \forall s$.

1.2 Tri-Modal Ingestion Pipeline (Modes A, B, and C)

Mode A: Full Origin-Destination Pairs (October Delhi Dataset)

When both start_stop_name and end_stop_name are recorded in the ETM transaction record:

$$b_s = \sum_{k} \mathbb{I}(\text{origin}_k = s) \cdot \text{passenger\_count}_k$$ $$a_s = \sum_{k} \mathbb{I}(\text{destination}_k = s) \cdot \text{passenger\_count}_k$$

Mode B: Boarding-Only Inference (Hybrid Alighting Model)

When only boarding stop $i$ is recorded, alighting probabilities $P(j \mid i)$ for downstream stops $j > i$ are estimated via convex combination:

$$P(j \mid i) = \alpha \cdot P_{\text{rev}}(j \mid i) + (1 - \alpha) \cdot P_{\text{grav}}(j \mid i)$$

Mode C: Daily Aggregate Disaggregation (Hare-Niemeyer Algorithm)

When only daily aggregate boardings $D_r$ are available, integer conservation is guaranteed by the largest remainder method:

$$\text{Quota}_t = D_r \times \frac{w_t}{\sum_{k=1}^M w_k}, \quad A_t = \lfloor \text{Quota}_t \rfloor, \quad R_t = \text{Quota}_t - A_t$$

1.3 Unrecorded Passenger Inflation (Pink Pass Multiplier)

To account for uncounted female commuters traveling with zero-fare Pink Passes:

$$b'_s = \gamma_r \times b_s, \quad \gamma_r \in [1.20, 1.45]$$

2. Spatial Graph & 8-Zone Corridor Partition

Each route pattern's stop sequence $s \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ is mapped into an 8-zone corridor representation:

$$z = \min\left(7, \left\lfloor \frac{8 \times s}{N} \right\rfloor\right) \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$$

3. Machine Learning Architecture & Confidence Estimation

3.1 Feature Pipeline

Category Features Formulation

Corridor Spatial zone_id, zone_id_norm Integer $z \in [0, 7]$ and normalized $z / 7.0$

3.2 Confidence Spread Metric

$$\text{Relative Spread} = \frac{p_{90} - p_{10}}{\max(\hat{y}, 0.1)}$$
  • High Confidence: Relative Spread < 0.35 and historical observations ≥ 10.
  • Medium Confidence: 0.35 ≤ Relative Spread < 0.75.
  • Low Confidence: Relative Spread ≥ 0.75 or zero historical observations.

4. Multi-Objective Journey Scoring

$$\text{Score} = w_{\text{time}} \cdot \frac{T - T_{\min}}{\max(1.0, T_{\max} - T_{\min})} + w_{\text{crowd}} \cdot \frac{\text{RCI}}{100.0}$$

Subject to the Detour Safeguard constraint: $T \le 1.5 \times T_{\text{fastest}} + 10\text{ min}$.
